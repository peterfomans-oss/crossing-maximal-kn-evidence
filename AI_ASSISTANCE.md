# AI assistance disclosure

AI systems, including ChatGPT/Codex, assisted this research and repository preparation. Assistance included:

- drafting and revising mathematical arguments;
- proposing proof strategies and counterexample searches;
- writing and reviewing enumeration, solver, certificate, and audit code;
- organizing run records and checking consistency between claims and evidence;
- preparing the English and Chinese repository documentation.

AI output was not treated as self-validating evidence. Claims in this repository are assigned evidence scopes in `CLAIMS.md`; finite enumerations and concrete objects are accompanied by programs or checkers where stated. The K17 UNSAT responses remain explicitly labeled as uncertified solver reports.

The local computations were executed on user-controlled hardware and their records were preserved. AI systems are not listed as human authors and do not replace author responsibility for the correctness, licensing, or publication of this material.

Known limitations of the workflow include the possibility of shared assumptions across generated code and reviews, incomplete formalization of prose arguments, and the absence of independently checkable proof traces for the two K17 solver runs. Independent expert review is welcomed.
